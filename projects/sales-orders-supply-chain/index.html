Sales, Orders & Supply Chain Dashboard

A Power BI report covering sales, orders, and suppliers, products and shipping.

View the .pbix on GitHub ↗

01Overview

The report is built on a relational model of orders, order details, products, categories, customers, suppliers, shippers and employees, with slicers and date hierarchies for filtering by region, category and time.

02What I built

  • Three report pages: Sales; Orders; Supplies, Products & Shipping
  • DAX measures: # Orders, Total Revenue, TotalSuppliers, and average, minimum and maximum processing time
  • Date hierarchies (year, quarter, month) and slicers on every page
