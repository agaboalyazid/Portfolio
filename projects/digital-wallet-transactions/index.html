Overview

The workbook summarises 5,000 transactions by device, status, category, merchant and month, with a location filter for urban, suburban and rural users.

What I built

  • KPI cards: transactions, total cashback, total fees, total paid amount, number of users
  • Charts by device, transaction status, top five categories, top five merchants and month
  • A location slicer for urban, suburban and rural users
